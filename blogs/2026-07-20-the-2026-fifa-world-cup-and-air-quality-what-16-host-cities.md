Photo by Sam Ladley on Unsplash The 2026 FIFA World Cup was the biggest in history: 48 teams, 104 matches, and 16 host cities spread across the United States, Mexico, and Canada. It was also one of the most environmentally challenging. From the June 11 opener to the July 19 final, the tournament ran straight through peak Western fire season and a record-warm North American summer.
